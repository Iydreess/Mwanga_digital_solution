Quick Answers

Frequently Asked
Questions

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?
+

We respond to all enquiries within 24 hours on business days (Mon–Fri). For urgent matters, WhatsApp is the fastest channel.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?
+

Absolutely. We serve clients across East Africa and globally. All project collaboration is handled remotely via video calls, email, and our project management platforms.

What information should I include in my project enquiry?
+

The more detail the better — your business type, what you need built, your timeline, your target audience, and your estimated budget. This helps us give you an accurate, tailored proposal.

Do you offer free consultations?
+

Yes — our initial discovery consultation is completely free. We'll discuss your goals, challenges, and how we can best help, with no obligation.

What payment methods do you accept?
+

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfers, PayPal, and major card payments. Projects are typically billed in milestones with an initial deposit to kick things off.
